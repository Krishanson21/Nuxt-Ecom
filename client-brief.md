# Slack Message from Sarah Chen (VP Product, Cadence)

#product-requests · 2 days ago

Hey team,

Our **customer engagement and repeat purchases aren't where we'd like them to be**. A lot of users browse products, place a single order, and never come back. There's **no sense of progression or reward** for shopping with us — one customer even mentioned, *"I’ve bought several products, but it feels like I’m starting from scratch every time I visit."*

I'd love us to add some **gamification** to the platform:

* A **reward points system** so customers earn points with every purchase, review, or completed profile action.
* **Customer levels or membership tiers** (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum) that unlock new perks as users continue shopping.
* **Shopping streaks** to encourage regular engagement — for example, users who make at least one purchase or complete a meaningful activity each week can maintain their streak and earn bonus rewards.
* Tie **product reviews, wishlists, referrals, and daily check-ins** into the rewards system so customers have incentives beyond just making purchases.
* Display each customer's **progress dashboard** showing their current points, tier, streak, and rewards earned.

**No public leaderboards or competitive rankings** — we want the experience to feel rewarding without comparing customers. Everything should remain **private to each user's account**.

Can someone scope this out? I'd like us to include it in this quarter's roadmap.

Sarah
